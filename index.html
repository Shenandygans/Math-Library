🧠 Problem of the Day

Prove that for $a,b,c>0$:

$$ \frac{a}{b+c}+\frac{b}{c+a}+\frac{c}{a+b}\ge \frac{3}{2} $$

Hint: Apply Cauchy–Schwarz (Titu's Lemma):

$$ \sum \frac{a}{b+c} = \sum \frac{a^2}{ab+ac} \ge \frac{(a+b+c)^2}{2(ab+bc+ca)} \ge \frac{3}{2}. $$
